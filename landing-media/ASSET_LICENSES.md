## Generated artwork

- `pet-ensemble.png` — original AI-generated soft-3D fallback artwork created for this project. It is displayed when interactive 3D is unavailable or reduced motion is preferred.

- `pet-trio.png` — dog, cat, and rabbit artwork generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for the 2026-10-14 landing page redesign.
- `pet-trio.webp` — optimized 1536 × 1024 web delivery copy of the same artwork, retaining transparency (423,336 bytes).

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent hero artwork for an existing veterinary clinic and pet care website. Create a beautifully art-directed, premium 3D illustration of exactly three friendly companion pets together: a happy golden retriever with natural floppy ears and a simple cobalt-blue collar, a curious soft gray-and-white cat, and a small cream rabbit. They sit closely together as a believable, charming group; the dog is largest, the cat about two-thirds its height, and the rabbit smallest. Give them warm expressive eyes, anatomically clear paws and ears, gentle rounded forms, finely textured matte fur, softly sculpted detail, and subtle realistic depth. Style: sophisticated contemporary 3D character rendering, lovable and polished, no exaggerated giant heads, no plastic shine. Composition: wide landscape group, front three-quarter view, all three entire bodies and paws fully visible, fill the frame generously with little empty margin, no cropping, no overlapped faces. Lighting: soft studio key light from upper left, bright natural warm highlights and cool gentle shadows. Natural golden and cream coats with the blue collar as the only vivid accent. True transparent background and clean alpha edges so the animals can sit against a light sky-blue website background; no background, no backdrop, no floor, no objects, no platform. No text, no logos, no watermark, no additional animals.
