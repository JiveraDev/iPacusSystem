### Retro Web · DESIGN.md Nostalgia

- [**Dell (1996)**](design-md/dell-1996/DESIGN.md) - Catalog-era enterprise web. Literal black page frame, flat color-block "ribbon cards", chunky Helvetica-Black titles over Times Roman body, and hand-cut GIF stickers (NEW! bursts, award seals, beveled product photos).
- [**Nintendo.com (2001)**](design-md/nintendo-2001/DESIGN.md) - Y2K "console chrome" web. Brushed-periwinkle beveled metal panels, a halftone-dotted carbon nav glowing amber, outlined Arial-Black box-art wordmarks over circuit-board hero fields, and a pixel Mario welcome bubble.
